About Our Department

Aeronautical Engineering deals with the design, development and testing of aircraft. It helps us understand how aircraft fly.

Wind Tunnel

A wind tunnel is used to study airflow around aircraft models. It helps engineers understand lift, drag and aerodynamic performance.

Aircraft Structure

The main parts of an aircraft are wings, fuselage, tail and landing gear. These parts provide lift, strength and stability.
